The Distribution Blueprint

Introduction



The Distribution Blueprint is India's pioneering free music distribution course, created by Saksham Tyagi, designed to provide independent artists and aspiring distributors with an in-depth understanding of the music distribution industry. With the growing demand for artists to manage their own music careers, this course serves as a comprehensive guide to help them take control of their music releases. Through a series of video lectures, tutorials, and resources, the course covers everything from the basics of digital music distribution to advanced strategies for global reach and monetization. The course provides essential tools, industry insights, and practical knowledge for artists to distribute their music across major streaming platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and more. By learning how to independently handle their music career, artists can unlock new opportunities for financial growth and artistic freedom.

Objective



The primary objective of The Distribution Blueprint is to empower independent artists by equipping them with the knowledge and tools necessary to navigate the complexities of the music distribution process. The course focuses on:

  • Music Distribution Basics: Teaching artists how to release their music across digital platforms, including step-by-step guides for uploading, managing releases, and ensuring maximum visibility.
  • Monetization Strategies: Educating artists on how to generate revenue from their music through streaming royalties, digital downloads, licensing opportunities, and more.
  • Promotion and Marketing: Providing actionable tips on how to effectively promote music releases and build a strong online presence. This includes social media strategies, playlist pitching, and leveraging digital marketing tools.
  • Independence from Labels: Helping artists gain full control of their careers by eliminating the need for a record label. The course empowers artists to release, promote, and monetize their music on their own terms.
  • Global Reach: Teaching artists how to distribute and promote their music globally, ensuring that they reach audiences beyond local or regional borders.
