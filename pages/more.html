Explore Our YouTube Playlist's

We value your input and want to create content that meets your needs! Please suggest a video title, idea, or topic that you'd like us to cover. Whether you're interested in any aspect of the music industry, feel free to request topics such as:


  • Music Distribution Step by Step: A complete guide on how to release your music from start to finish, covering distributor selection, uploading, metadata, and release strategies.

  • Music Distribution Problems: Common issues artists face while distributing music, including rejection reasons, delays, and platform-specific problems, along with solutions.

  • Music License (Content ID): Understanding how Content ID works, protecting your music from unauthorized use, managing claims, and earning from YouTube's Content ID system.

  • Artist Accounts: A step-by-step guide on setting up and managing artist accounts on platforms like Spotify for Artists, Apple Music for Artists, and Amazon Music for Artists.

  • OAC (Official Artist Channel): How to claim and verify your Official Artist Channel (OAC) on YouTube, its benefits, and how to manage it effectively.

  • Song Upload on Google Websites: A guide to uploading and distributing your music on Google's websites.

  • How to Upload Song on Label: A detailed explanation of the process of releasing music under a record label, the benefits, and how to pitch your songs to labels.

  • Music Distribution Earnings: How artists can earn money from streaming, royalties, licensing, and digital downloads through music distribution platforms.

  • Copyright Issue: Understanding copyright laws, how to protect your music legally, and how to resolve copyright strikes and claims.

  • Lofi, Slowed+Reverb, Remix, Beats & Cover Distribution: A special section dedicated to distributing LoFi, Slowed+Reverb, Remix, Beats & Cover music, including strategies for marketing, playlist pitching, and monetization.

  • Music by AI: Exploring AI-generated music—how to distribute, monetize, and navigate copyright concerns in the evolving AI music industry.

And any other music-related topics you want to learn about! We are always open to your ideas. Feel free to suggest anything—from music production tips to navigating the business side of the music industry. Let us know, and we’ll make sure to create a video that’s tailored to your needs.


For video requests, drop us an email at contact@distributionblueprint.in, and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible!
