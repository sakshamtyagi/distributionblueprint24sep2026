FAQ - The Distribution Blueprint

What is The Distribution Blueprint?

The Distribution Blueprint is India's first free music distribution platform, created to empower independent artists and aspiring distributors with the knowledge needed to distribute music effectively.

Who is this Platform for?

This Platform is designed for independent artists, music producers, and aspiring music distributors who want to learn how to distribute their music to streaming platforms without the need for a label.

What are covered in the Platform?

The Platform covers topics such as music rights, distribution platforms, metadata management, release strategies, and revenue collection.

How can I enroll in Distribution Blueprint Community?

Is there a certification for completing the course?

Yes, upon completing all the modules in The Distribution Blueprint, you will generate your certificate on Get your certificate button.

What music platforms will I learn to distribute my music on?

You will learn how to distribute your music on major platforms such as Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, YouTube, and more.

Do I need any prior experience to take this course?

No prior experience is needed. This course is designed to be beginner-friendly and will guide you through all the steps of music distribution.

How long does it take to complete the course?

The course is self-paced, so you can complete it in your own time. Most learners finish within a few weeks, depending on their schedule.

What will I learn about music metadata?

You will learn how to correctly input and manage metadata like ISRC codes, song titles, artist names, and genre to ensure your music is properly categorized on platforms.

Does the course cover music promotion strategies?

Yes, the course includes a section on how to promote your music effectively after distribution, including tips for social media marketing and playlist placements.

Will I learn about collecting royalties?

Yes, you will learn how to collect royalties from streaming platforms, including performance royalties, mechanical royalties, and how to set up your accounts.

Is this course suitable for producers as well?

Yes, producers can also benefit from this course, as it covers the full process of music distribution, which includes getting production credits and royalties.

Can I take this course if I’m not based in India?

Yes, this course is available worldwide, and the principles of music distribution apply universally, no matter where you're located.

Do I need special software to complete the course?

No special software is required. All you need is an internet connection and a device to access the course materials.

You can always reach out to our support team via email or join our online community for additional guidance.

No, The Distribution Blueprint is a free course with no hidden fees or extra costs.
