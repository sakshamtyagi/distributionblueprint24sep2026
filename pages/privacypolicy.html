Introduction

Welcome to the official website. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy outlines the types of personal information we may collect and how we use it when you visit our website or interact with our services.

We collect information from you when you visit our site, fill out forms, or interact with other content on our website. The types of personal information we may collect include:

  • Personal Identification Information (e.g., Name, Email Address).
  • Non-personal identification information (e.g., browser type, internet service provider).
  • Payment information, if you donate or make any transaction (processed through secure payment platforms, not stored directly by us).
  • Feedback, comments, or messages you send to us through the website.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect from you may be used in the following ways:

  • To personalize your experience and respond to your individual needs.
  • To improve our website, based on the feedback and interactions you provide.
  • To process your transactions securely.
  • To respond to your questions, feedback, or support requests promptly.
  • To send periodic emails regarding course updates, promotions, or other content that we believe may interest you (with your consent).

Security of Your Information

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, we recommend being cautious about sharing sensitive information online, as no method of data transmission over the internet is fully secure. For sensitive transactions, such as payments, we use secure payment processing platforms.

By using our website, you consent to our privacy policy. If at any time you wish to withdraw your consent or have questions about our use of your personal information, you can reach out to us. You can also opt-out of receiving marketing emails by following the instructions included in the emails.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance the user experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us track usage patterns, preferences, and improve site functionality. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

Third-party Links

Occasionally, we may include third-party links on our website for relevant content or services. These third-party sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. Please review their privacy policies before interacting with those sites.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data. You can contact us through our Contact Page to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or change this privacy policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with the effective date of the updated policy. Please check back periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your personal information.
